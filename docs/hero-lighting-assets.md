# Registered hero lighting assets

The master `public/images/premium-home/hero-dubai.webp` is unchanged. Both derived files are lossless WebP at the same 2172 × 724 resolution:

- `public/images/premium-home/hero-dubai-off.webp`: the original photograph with localized diffuser shading baked into its pixels.
- `public/images/premium-home/hero-dubai-on.webp`: the original pixels with localized, material-aware illumination. The diffuser retains its contour and brightness; the white housing, metal ridges, adjacent wall and marble receive visible, localized reflected light. The wall includes a soft diffuse response so the saturated red visibly reacts. Housing illumination falls off without disappearing at the lower product, while the perspective-aligned marble reflection is stronger and slightly wider.

The built-in image editing tool produced a lighting reference. Its skyline and detail differed from the original, so it was **not** used as a replacement photograph. Only a blurred, low-resolution RGB exposure field was extracted. No generated edges, lettering, objects or textures enter the final assets. The original image is never resized, warped, blurred or shifted during the retouch. This prevents geometry drift and double edges during crossfading.

`scripts/build-hero-lighting.mjs` applies that field through material masks to the original pixels in linear-light color. Dark lettering and chrome grooves are protected; stone reflection follows the platform perspective and preserves the contact shadow. Wall illumination scales the existing pigment rather than inserting an opaque colored gradient. The reference field is stored in `scripts/assets/hero-lighting-field.png` (136 × 46), with verification in `scripts/assets/hero-lighting-report.json`.

Rebuild deterministically with `node scripts/build-hero-lighting.mjs`. To replace only the lighting reference, pass a 2172 × 724 reference image as the optional argument. The script checks unchanged pixels outside the localized region and lossless round-trip decoding. The left headline area, battery, distant skyline, wall geometry and plant remain unchanged.

Runtime uses the existing responsive source plane for both states. The same ON image is used for the leading diffuser fade and full-scene fade, so overlap cannot over-brighten it. The diffuser starts at 0ms and settles at 1050ms; the full scene starts at 650ms and settles at 1800ms. OFF has no delay and takes 600ms. Both assets decode before ignition. Manual intent while loading, rapid reversal, keyboard operation and reduced-motion preferences are supported. The only additional runtime light is the existing restrained hover accent.
